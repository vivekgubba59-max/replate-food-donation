About RePlate — Food Donation Platform

A simple, beginner-friendly platform built to solve local food waste and hunger.

🛑

The Problem

Over 30% of all food is thrown into trash bins every day after hotel banquets, restaurant services, and campus events. At the same time, nearby shelters, hostels, and families go to bed hungry.

The problem is not a shortage of food—it is the lack of a simple, quick way to notify nearby charities before fresh food spoils.

🌱

Our Solution: RePlate

RePlate makes food rescue as simple as posting a message:

  • 🍲 Donors post extra food in under 30 seconds.
  • 🤝 Charities & NGOs view and request food with 1 click.
  • 🚚 Volunteers pick up the food and share it directly.
  • 📊 Dashboard tracks total meals and environmental impact live.

🧠 How NGO Matching Works (In Simple English)

When an extra food donation is posted, RePlate calculates the best NGO partner using 4 fair and transparent rules:

1. Distance (35%)

Shelters closest to the donor get higher scores so food arrives fast and hot.

2. Urgency (25%)

If food expires in under 2 hours, nearby NGOs are prioritized to prevent waste.

3. Vehicle Capacity (20%)

Large donations are matched with organizations that have vans to transport them.

4. Food Category (15%)

Matches cooked food to hot kitchens, and cold produce to refrigerator-equipped pantries.
