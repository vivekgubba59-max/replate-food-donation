Deterministic Matching Algorithm Architecture

Transparent, explainable multi-factor scoring ensuring safe, rapid, and auditable food rescue dispatches.

35% Weight
📍 Proximity & Transit Distance
Calculated via Earth-curvature Haversine Distance between donor GPS and NGO headquarters. Full score within 2 km; decay curve up to maximum fleet radius. Eliminates excessive transit times.
25% Weight
⏰ Shelf-Life Urgency & ETA
Evaluates remaining food safety window against estimated rescue arrival time (8m mobilization + 2.5m/km transit). For donations expiring in under 2 hours, distant NGOs are penalized to prevent spoilage in traffic.
20% Weight
🚚 Vehicle & Batch Capacity
Compares donation volume against NGO intake capacity. Rewards ideal single-run load utilization (30% to 100% capacity) while flagging overloaded trips that would require multiple vehicles.
15% Weight
🥗 Category Alignment & Equipment
Matches food categories (Cooked Hot Meals, Fresh Cold Produce, Bakery, Dairy) with NGO distribution facilities (e.g. thermal hot boxes vs cold-chain refrigeration units).
5% Weight
⭐ Fulfillment Reliability
Incentivizes dependable community partners based on historical pickup promptness and verified beneficiary distribution rates.

💡 Why RePlate Chooses Explainable Heuristics Over Black-Box Models

In urgent food logistics, opaque neural predictions present major safety and compliance liabilities. Our deterministic scoring model guarantees:

Zero Hallucinations:

No hallucinated routes, fake pickup times, or non-existent recipient shelters.

Food Safety Compliance:

Strict mathematical limits ensure cooked items are never dispatched if the transit ETA exceeds the temperature safety deadline.

100% Auditability:

Every NGO score provides a clear breakdown (+35 pts distance, +25 pts urgency) ready for public grants and municipal inspections.
